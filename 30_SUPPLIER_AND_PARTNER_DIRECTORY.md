# 30. Supplier & Partner Directory

**Purpose:** One-page directories for machines, materials, and partnership contacts. Update every term; share with partner schools.

---

## 1. Machine & Consumables Suppliers

| Item | Suggested source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Heat press 8-in-1 | Industrial/office supply | Budget model is fine for HDPE sheets |
| Paper shredder (for PLASTIC ONLY) | Heavy-duty office | Keep a dedicated machine, never mixed with paper |
| Laser cutter (e.g., K40) | Fab lab supplier | Ventilation mandatory — see doc 04 |
| Injection molder | Plastic recycling hobby kit | E.g., "Precious Plastic" style small molder |
| Parchment paper rolls | Bulk grocery | Non-stick sheets for the press |
| Silicone/foam cap liners (removed) | Compost program | Liners go to compost/other recycling, never melt |
| Food-safe HDPE pellets (optional) | Recycled plastic supplier | For consistent QC batches |

Duty and import taxes can vary; ask for a formal quote per school requisition (doc 13).

---

## 2. Cap Sourcing Partners

| Source | Volume estimate | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| Cafeteria & canteen | 200 caps/day if school-wide | Student logistic crew |
| Local cafes & bars (cork/normal caps) | 500 caps/month each | Parent volunteers |
| Pharmacy chains (medicine bottle caps) | 300 caps/month | Community drives |
| Events (marathons, fairs) | 1,000+ per event | Launch-week team (doc 19) |

Create a **per-partner log** (doc 03 Command Center sheet): partner, contact, weekly target, collected + verified counts.

---

## 3. Community Business Partners

- **Collector:** local waste/recycling coordinators for overflow bags.
- **Manufacturer:** a local plastics workshop willing to trade knowledge for a student showcase.
- **Retail:** small shops that host a drop bin in exchange for a "proud partner" poster.
- **Media:** school newsletter, city paper — news beats earn goodwill and more donors.

### Partnership Letter Template

```
Dear [Partner],

The Cap Collective ([school]) turns discarded bottle caps into products and learning.
We'd love to invite [Partner] to join as a cap collection point or in-kind supplier.
Benefits to you: recognize a cause your customers love, get quarterly impact reports,
and appear in our community partner wall.

We can drop off a collection bin and posters this week. What works best for you?

With gratitude,
The Cap Collective Team
```

---

## 4. Renewal & Governance

- Review the directory **every 8 weeks** (each term) at the Command Center meeting.
- Record who owns each relationship — never a single student as sole contact.
- All in-kind donations get a thank-you postcard + entry in the impact report (doc 23).

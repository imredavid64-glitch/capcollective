| Waste diverted | calc | kg |

**Key conversions:**
- 1 cap ≈ 2.5 g
- CO₂e avoided = kg × 1.9
- Virgin HDPE energy ≈ 75 MJ/kg (vs ~10 for recycled)

# PART 4: ANNUAL REPORT (5-8 Pages, Public-Facing)

## Suggested Structure
1. **Letter from the students** (1 page, student voice)
2. **Year in review** — narrative + timeline
3. **Impact dashboard** — full-year data with charts
4. **Financial statement** — income/expenses, balance
5. **Learning & curriculum outcomes**
6. **Community & partnerships**
7. **Media coverage** (thumbnails + links)
8. **Testimonials**
9. **Sustainability plan** (how it continues)
10. **Call to action** (donate / partner / replicate)

# PART 7: VISUALIZATION CHEAT-SHEET
| Message | Best chart |
|---------|-----------|
| Growth over time | Line chart |
| Comparing categories | Bar chart |
| Composition | Pie/donut |
| Goal progress | Gauge / progress bar |
| Before vs after | Two-column compare |
| Impact equivalence | Icon array (e.g., trees) |

**Impact equivalence bank (for storytelling):**
- 1 kg CO₂e ≈ 1 tree absorbing CO₂ for ~50 days
- 65 kg CO₂e ≈ 2.6 trees for a year (≈ 65/25)
- 1 kg plastic ≈ 2 L of oil saved (virgin production)
- 10,000 caps ≈ 25 kg of plastic kept out of landfill

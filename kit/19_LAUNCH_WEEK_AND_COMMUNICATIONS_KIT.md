# PART 2: SCRIPTS

## SCRIPT A — LAUNCH ASSEMBLY (5-7 min)
*(Two students + Program Lead on stage. Cap in a jar as a prop.)*

**[Student 1]:** "Good morning everyone. How many of you drank something from a plastic bottle this week?"
*(hands)*
"That's a LOT. Here's the thing — the bottle usually gets recycled. But the CAP? Usually not."

**[Student 2]:** "Only 3 percent of bottle caps get recycled. The rest end up in landfill — or the ocean. Sea turtles and fish eat them because they look like food."

**[Student 1]:** "So we're doing something about it. Starting today, bottle caps have a new job at this school."

**[Program Lead]:** "Introducing Cap Collective. [X] collection boxes are now around campus. Every cap you drop in gets counted, washed, and turned into something new — coasters, organizers, plant pots. And the money funds our garden."

**[Student 2]:** "Here's how you can help: 1 — find a cap. 2 — drop it in a box. 3 — watch our live count go up."

**[Student 1]:** "We even have a machine that counts every cap automatically. You can see the number live on the screen right now."
*(show dashboard)*

**[Program Lead]:** "This Friday is our first Collection Day. The class with the most caps wins [prize]. Let's build the bridge. Join the Bridge. Create the Impact."
*(applause)*

## SCRIPT B — PA ANNOUNCEMENTS (30 sec each)
**PA-1 (Monday):**
> "Good morning! Cap Collective launches today. Look for the big wooden boxes — drop your bottle caps in. Every cap counts. Let's go!"

**PA-2 (Friday):**
> "It's the FIRST Cap Drop Day! Bring your caps from home and school. The winning class gets [prize]. Drop. Count. Repeat!"

**PA-3 (Weekly recurring):**
> "Cap Collective update: we've collected [X] caps — that's [Y] kilos kept out of landfill. Keep it up!"

**PA-4 (Store launch):**
> "Our Eco-Store is open! Student-made coasters, keychains, and organizers — made from YOUR caps. Find us at [location]."

## SCRIPT C — FIRST CAP DROP CEREMONY (Friday, 10 min)
```
1. Program Lead welcomes everyone to the main box.
2. A student leader holds up the "Golden Cap" (spray-painted cap).
3. Principal drops the GOLDEN CAP into the box first — the symbolic first cap.
4. Students cheer; sensor counts it; projector shows the number = 1.
5. Each class rep drops a handful.
6. Announce the competition + prize.
7. Group photo for comms.
```

# PART 3: PRESS & MEDIA

## PRESS RELEASE TEMPLATE
```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
[Date]

STUDENTS TURN BOTTLE CAPS INTO PRODUCTS AT [SCHOOL]
New Student-Led Program Diverts Plastic Waste and Funds the Garden

[City, Date] — [School Name] today launched Cap Collective, a
student-run program that collects bottle caps and transforms them
into sellable products such as coasters, organizers, and plant pots.

Only about 3% of bottle caps are recycled globally, and because of
their small size they often escape industrial sorting and end up in
landfills or oceans. Cap Collective captures caps at the source,
using a five-step process: collect, clean, shred, melt, and refine.

"Students aren't just learning about sustainability — they're building
it," said [Program Lead]. "Every cap they drop in becomes a product,
and every product funds our garden."

In its first semester, the program aims to divert more than 10,000
caps (35+ kg), certify [X] students, and generate $500+ in revenue.

[Quote from Principal.]

MEDIA CONTACT: [Name, email, phone]
PHOTO/VIDEO OPPORTUNITIES: [dates/times]
```

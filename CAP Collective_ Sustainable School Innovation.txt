Environmental Reality
The Plastic Crisis
Over 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans annually. Bottle caps are consistently ranked among the top 5 deadliest marine debris items found during coastal cleanups.

Why Focus on Bottle Caps?
Ease of Sorting
Caps are made of High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE), which is easily separated from standard PET water bottles.
Value Density
Small but highly recyclable. HDPE holds its physical properties well during the melting and reshaping process.
Industrial Versatility
Easy to clean, melt, and shred, making it the perfect "raw material" for classroom-scale manufacturing.

The Transformation Cycle
Collect
Sorting & Cleaning Grade 2 HDPE plastics.
Shred
Breaking down caps into uniform plastic flakes.
Melt
Heat transformation into workable sheets.
Refine
Laser cutting or 3D printing into products.

Technology Integration
Sensor Integration
Utilizing IR (Infrared) "beam-break" sensors to automate counting. Data is sent to an Arduino or ESP32 to monitor real-time bin levels and collection speed.
Data Analysis App
Students develop custom dashboards to visualize their carbon footprint reduction and material throughput. Bridging Computer Science with Sustainability.

School Implementation Plan
Identify Hubs: Select high-traffic collection zones (Cafeteria, Entry Hall).
Workshop Setup: Assign ventilated workspace for shredders and heat presses.
Curriculum Integration: Map project to Science, Art, and Math double-periods.
Eco-Store Launch: Sell recycled products locally to fund future projects.

# 31. Train-the-Trainer & Ambassador Program

**Purpose:** Make the program self-sustaining by certifying students who certify others and by turning your top students into public ambassadors.

---

## 1. Why Train-the-Trainer?

A program that only runs when the founder teacher is present dies at graduation. Building a trainer tier gives you:

| Benefit | Result |
|---|---|
| Certified senior students | Machines run during club hours without the teacher |
| Student trainers | Other students learn faster from peers |
| Handover pipeline | Autonomous operation by the next cohort |

---

## 2. The Trainer Tier (green +)

Eligibility:

- 2 semesters in the program OR 50+ verified logged working hours.
- Foundation Safety Certificate (doc 17 / try the quiz at `learn.html`).
- Pass a **practical cascade**: the student runs a full batch (collect → clean → shred → melt → refine) with the teacher signing off an observation checklist.

### Trainer Duties

- Supervise one machine station during club hours.
- Onboard & observe new card candidates.
- Update the Command Center sheets (doc 03).
- Report near-misses immediately to the staff sponsor.

### Trainer Limits

- A trainer **cannot** clear a fault alone — a staff member is called for any jam, smoke, or alarm.
- Trainers train only on their own certified machine card(s).

---

## 3. Ambassador Program (outward-facing)

Ambassadors speak and demo for the school community. Roles:

1. **Storyteller** — runs the Instagram/photo diary and the impact gallery page.
2. **Demo Captain** — hosts launch-week stalls and open days.
3. **Partner Liaison** — maintains the supplier directory (doc 30) contacts.
4. **Eco-Store Manager** — runs inventory + client checkouts (doc 21).

### Ambassador Principles

- **Script first, ad-lib second:** use the launch-deck speaking notes (doc 11).
- **No safety shortcuts in demos:** observers get a verbal mini-safety brief before hands-on.
- **One adult present at every public event.**

- Review the roster each term for expiring cards (retest if > 12 months).

---

## 5. Measuring Success

Track over the year:

- Number of student trainers active.
- Number of certifications issued by trainers (not staff).
- Ambassador-led events and visits.
- % of club operations run without staff in the room (target 40% by end of year 2).

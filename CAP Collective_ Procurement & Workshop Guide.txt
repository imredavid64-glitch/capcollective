IMPLEMENTATION PLANS
Select the plan that fits your organization's budget and technical goals. Start small and scale up.

Ask for Educational Discounts: Use keywords like "Schulrabatt" (DE) or "Remise Éducative" (FR) for VAT-exempt or B2B pricing.
Prioritize Auto-Open: Magnetic locks on heat presses prevent scorched projects and increase safety during student distractions.
Starter Kits: Look for "Press + Printer" bundles from distributors like Brildor to save up to 15% on separate components.
Compliance: Ensure all machinery is CE-certified and includes EU-standard safety guards.
SMART PROCUREMENT TIPS

Material Identification
Only collect Grade 2 (HDPE) or Grade 5 (PP) caps. Mixes will cause structural failure in the final product.
Washing & Drying
Wash in warm soapy water. Residual milk or sugar creates toxic fumes and "burn marks" during heating.
Removing Liners
Crucial: Pull out the clear silicone liners from inside the caps. These do not melt and ruin the mold.
STEP 1: COLLECTION & PREP

Creating the Flake
Uniform shred size (5mm - 8mm) ensures an even melt. Large chunks create air pockets (voids) in the material.
•
Feeding: Drop caps slowly to prevent motor jam.
•
Sieving: Pass flakes through a mesh screen to ensure consistency.
•
Color Mixing: Pre-mix flakes in buckets to create marbling or solid patterns before melting.
STEP 2: MATERIAL SHREDDING

Temperature Setting
Set to 140°C for HDPE. Wait for the platen to reach full temp before starting.
The Parchment Sandwich
Place flakes between two sheets of parchment paper (baking paper). Never let plastic touch the metal platen.
Pressing Cycle
Press for 5 mins, flip the "sandwich," and press for another 5 mins for a perfectly smooth sheet.
METHOD: HEAT PRESS OPERATION

Injection SOP
Best for 3D objects like keychains or small tools.
•
Pre-heat Barrel: Set barrel to 180°C (higher than press due to barrel friction).
•
Clamping: Secure the mold tightly under the nozzle using a C-clamp.
•
Injection: Pull the lever with steady, firm pressure until it stops.
•
Demolding: Wait 2-3 mins for plastic to solidify before opening the mold.

WORKSHOP ESSENTIAL TOOLS

Institutional Discounts: Always ask for "Educational Pricing" or VAT-exempt status for public schools.
Ventilation: Budget for air extraction. Even "safe" plastics require active air circulation in classrooms.
Maintenance: Buy shredder oil sheets and heat-press platen cleaners to extend machine life.
Power: Ensure your workshop has 16A circuits; heat presses and shredders can pull significant amperage during warm-up.

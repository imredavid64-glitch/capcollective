# Cap Collective — Engagement & Gamification Guide
### Keep Students Hooked for the Whole Year
**Points, competitions, badges, and behavioral design — with fairness guardrails.**

---

# PART 1: WHY GAMIFY
Recycling programs fade because the payoff is invisible and slow. Gamification fixes that with **fast feedback**, **friendly competition**, and **status**. Every mechanic below is optional — pick what fits your culture.

---

# PART 2: POINTS SYSTEM (Core Loop)

## How to Earn Points
| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Drop in 1 cap | 1 |
| Bring a full bag from home | 25 |
| Volunteer 1 session | 50 |
| Complete a certification | 100 |
| Make 1 product | 20 |
| Sell 1 product | 30 |
| Bring a NEW participant | 40 |
| Post to social (approved) | 25 |
| Win a weekly challenge | 150 |

## How Points Are Used
- **Class leaderboard** (primary)
- **Individual levels** (badges)
- **Redeem** for privileges (see Rewards)

**Rule:** Class competition is the main driver (avoids bullying/hoarding); individuals still earn badges.

# PART 3: CLASS / HOUSE COMPETITION

## Structure Options
| Format | Best for | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Class vs class | Elementary | Per-capita scoring |
| House system | Secondary | Mixes grades, builds community |
| Grade vs grade | Middle | Simpler |

## Scoring — Use **Per-Capita** (fairness!)
```
Class score = total caps ÷ number of students in class
```
This lets a small class win — critical for inclusion.

## Competition Calendar (Term)
| Week | Challenge |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Launch score (all count) |
| 3 | "Bring-from-home" week |
| 5 | "Golden Cap" hidden-cap hunt |
| 7 | Faculty vs students week |
| 9 | "Design a product" vote |
| 11 | Final sprint (double points Friday) |
| 12 | Awards assembly |

## Leaderboard Display
- Physical: whiteboard in cafeteria
- Digital: live on the dashboard
- Weekly: PA announcement

# PART 5: STREAKS, QUESTS & MOMENTS
- **Collection streak:** consecutive days with ≥1 cap — show flame icon.
- **Weekly quest:** "Collect 500 caps as a school." Progress bar shown.
- **Surprise doubling:** randomly announce "DOUBLE POINTS UNTIL 1PM!" via PA.
- **Golden Cap:** a spray-painted cap worth 500 points, hidden in the pile; whoever finds it (during sorting) scores big.
- **Mystery Monday:** random participating class gets bonus points.

# PART 7: BEHAVIORAL DESIGN NUDGES (Make it easy)

Based on proven behavioral science:

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Make it easy** | Boxes in high-traffic spots; wide drop slot |
| **Make it visible** | Live counter everyone passes |
| **Social proof** | "Your class is 2nd — 40 behind!" |
| **Fresh start effect** | New goal every Monday |
| **Loss aversion** | Progress bars that can "drop" if you slack |
| **Identity** | "You're a Maker now" language |
| **Immediate reward** | Instant points/ding on the dashboard |
| **Commitment** | Students sign a "collector pledge" |
| **Anchoring** | Show the year goal big; small steps beside it |

# PART 9: FAIRNESS & INCLUSION GUARDRAILS
**Competition can hurt if it shames or excludes. Prevent that:**
- Score **per capita**, not raw totals.
- **Rotate winners** — bonus multipliers to trailing classes.
- **Everyone can earn badges** regardless of volume.
- Never publicly show the *lowest* class.
- Provide caps/opportunities to students who can't collect at home.
- Celebrate effort ("Most Improved") as much as output.

# PART 10: RECOGNITION WALL & CEREMONY
- **Wall of Impact:** names + photos of badge earners.
- **Termly awards assembly:** hand out certificates (`05_PRINTABLE_RESOURCES.md`).
- Categories: Top Collector, Most Improved, Best Maker, Best Recruiter, Safety Star, Team of the Term.

# PART 11: MEASURING ENGAGEMENT
| Metric | Target | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Weekly active participants | 40%+ | Roster |
| Avg caps/student | 20+/term | Tracker |
| Retention (still active wk 8) | 60%+ | Roster |
| Badges earned | 100+/term | Badge log |
| Event attendance | 80% of roster | Sign-in |

**If engagement drops:** add surprise doubling, a mystery cap, or a faculty-vs-student week. Momentum beats polish.

    FAQ & Troubleshooting¶

    Frequently asked questions and solutions for Cap Collective operations.

    Getting Started¶

    Q: How much does it cost to start?¶

    A: Three budget tiers: - Core: $500-1,000 (press, hand tools, PPE, manual shredder) - FabLab: $2,500-5,000 (auto press, 3D printer, sensors, GDPR shredder) - Center: $10,000+ (injection molder, laser, CNC, ventilation)

    Start Core. Scale up. Every tier produces real products.

    Q: Do I need a dedicated room?¶

    A: Minimum 200 sq ft with: - Exterior wall for ventilation (100+ CFM exhaust) - 16A dedicated circuit (press) - Sink access (wash station) - Fire extinguisher + blanket + first aid

    Q: What grades can participate?¶

    A: K-12 with differentiated roles: - K-2: Cap Detectives (sort, count, decorate) - 3-5: Cap Engineers (build boxes, wash/shred supervised, Tinkercad) - 6-8: Cap Innovators (Arduino, Fusion 360, business plan) - 9-12: Systems Designers (LCA, CNC, grants, B2B)

    Q: How long before we make products?¶

    A: Typical timeline: - Weeks 1-2: Approvals, equipment arrival, safety certs - Weeks 3-4: Box building, first collection, wash/shred demo - Weeks 5-8: First sheets pressed, first coasters cut - Week 9+: Product sales, eco-store launch

    Equipment & Operations¶

    Q: What plastic caps work?¶

    A: Only HDPE #2 and PP #5: - ✅ Milk jug caps (HDPE #2) - ✅ Water bottle caps (HDPE #2) - ✅ Detergent caps (HDPE #2) - ✅ Yogurt cups (PP #5) - ✅ Ketchup/mayo caps (PP #5) - ✅ Medicine bottle caps (PP #5) - ❌ PET #1 bottles (sinks, different melt) - ❌ Metal caps - ❌ Caps with liners still in - ❌ Dirty/contaminated caps

    Q: How do I identify HDPE vs PP?¶

    A: - Float test: HDPE floats in water, PP sinks (barely) - Look for symbol: #2 = HDPE, #5 = PP - Feel: HDPE softer, PP more rigid - Burn test (teachers only): HDPE = candle wax smell, PP = diesel smell

    Q: What temperature for pressing?¶

    A: - HDPE: 140°C (284°F) — 5 min + flip + 5 min - PP: 170°C (338°F) — 4 min + flip + 4 min - MAX: 200°C (NEVER EXCEED) - Always verify with IR thermometer

    Q: Why do my sheets warp?¶

    A: Common causes: - Not cooling under weight (10 min minimum) - Uneven flake distribution - Parchment not clean/flat - Temp too high / time too long - Removed before fully cool

    Fix: Cool 10+ min under flat weight (steel plate works)

    Q: Why are my sheets burnt/discolored?¶

    A: - Temp too high → Lower 5-10°C - Time too long → Reduce 1 min per side - Parchment reused too many times → Fresh parchment - Flakes too thick → Spread thinner (~3mm)

    Q: Why do my coasters crack/break?¶

    A: - Sheet too thin (<2.5mm) → Press thicker - Edges not sanded → Sand all edges - Material too brittle (old/oxidized flakes) → Fresher flakes - Design has sharp internal corners → Add 0.5mm fillets

    Q: Shredder keeps jamming. Help!¶

    A: 1. REVERSE → STOP → WAIT 30 SEC → Clear with stick (NEVER hands) 2. Feed slower, one handful at a time 3. Check sieve: 5-8mm only (no fines, no chunks) 4. Oil blades weekly (shredder oil sheets) 5. Check for metal contamination (magnet on input)

    Q: Laser cuts are messy/burnt.¶

    A: - Focus test first (ramp test or material test) - Air assist ON (mandatory for HDPE) - Settings for 3mm HDPE (60W CO2): 60% power, 20mm/s, 2 passes - Frame check before every cut - Watch ENTIRE cut — never leave - 30 sec wait after cut before opening

    Q: Sensor not sending data.¶

    A: 1. Check WiFi credentials (2.4GHz only!) 2. Verify Apps Script URL (redeploy as "Anyone") 3. Check Serial Monitor for error codes 4. Verify bin_id matches Command Center 5. Check GPIO pin matches code (default 27) 6. Restart ESP32 (power cycle)

    Safety & Compliance¶

    Q: What PPE is required?¶

    A: Per station: | Station | Goggles | Heat Gloves | N95+ | Apron | Ear Pro | |---------|---------|-------------|------|-------|---------| | Wash | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ | | Shredder | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | | Press | ✅ | ✅ 500°F | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | | Laser | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | | Injection | ✅ | ✅ 500°F | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

    Q: What age for machines?¶

    A: - Shredder/Press/Laser/Injection: 14+ with certification - CNC: 16+ with certification - 3D Printer: 12+ with supervision - Wash/Wash/Sort: Any age (with supervision)

    Q: Do students need parental consent?¶

    A: Yes. Forms required: 1. Program Consent (all activities) 2. Safety Pledge (student signed) 3. Photo/Video Release (opt-out available) 4. Medical Info (confidential) 5. Machine-specific consent (for 14+ machines)

    Q: What insurance do we need?¶

    A: Typically covered under school general liability. Verify: - [ ] Student activities endorsement - [ ] Equipment coverage - [ ] Product liability (for sales) - [ ] Volunteer coverage - [ ] Field trip/event endorsement

    Q: What about fumes?¶

    A: - HDPE/PP at 140-170°C = minimal fumes (below degradation temp) - Never exceed 200°C (degradation starts ~250°C) - Ventilation mandatory: 100+ CFM at press - N95+ required at press/shredder/laser - CO2 monitor recommended (alert >1000 ppm)

    Data & Dashboard¶

    Q: How does the dashboard work?¶

    A: Two options: - Option A (Sheets + Looker): Free, 15-min refresh, no code - Option B (Supabase + Next.js): Real-time, custom, CS project - Option C (HTML/JS + Supabase): Real-time, no build

    Q: How do I connect sensors to Google Sheets?¶

    A: 1. ESP32 + FC-51 sensor → WiFi → Apps Script webhook 2. Apps Script → appends to "Sensor Data Log" tab 3. Formulas in "Impact Dashboard" tab auto-calculate 5. Looker Studio connects to Sheet → charts

    Q: Can I use the dashboard without sensors?¶

    A: Yes! Manual entry in Collection Tracker tab works perfectly. Sensors just automate counting.

    Q: How do I publish the Command Center as CSV?¶

    A: File → Share → Publish to web → Select tab → CSV → Copy URL → Use in dashboard config

    Q: Dashboard shows old data.¶

    A: - Google Sheets caches (open sheet to refresh) - Looker: Refresh data source or wait 15 min - Supabase: Check realtime subscription active - Check sensor battery/WiFi

    Products & Sales¶

    Q: What products sell best?¶

    A: Ranked by margin × velocity: 1. Coaster 4-pack — $12, 70% margin, high volume 2. Keychains — $4, 80% margin, impulse buy 3. Desk Organizers — $18, 65% margin, gift market 4. Keychain/Badge — $3-5, 80% margin, events 5. Outdoor Tiles — $25, 60% margin, B2B

    Q: How do I price products?¶

    A: Formula: (Material + Energy + Packaging) ÷ (1 - Target Margin) - Target 65-70% margin - Include: flakes, parchment, energy, packaging, labor ($0 student) - Round to psychological price ($12 not $11.99)

    Q: Can we sell online?¶

    A: Yes, with compliance: - Shopify/Etsy: Marketplace facilitator collects sales tax - Local pickup only (sustainability — no shipping plastic) - Digital products: Curriculum packs, design files (100% margin) - Compliance: Sales tax nexus, business license (usually under school/PTA)

    Q: How do we handle cash?¶

    A: - Square Reader (2.6% + $0.10) — auto-syncs to dashboard - Cash box: $50 float ($20 ones, $10 fives, $20 change) - Dual count: 2 students + teacher verify open/close - Daily deposit log in Command Center Tab 3

    Q: What if a product fails QC?¶

    A: 1. Document: photo + batch # + failure mode 2. Root cause (5 Whys) 3. Re-shred → Re-press → Re-test 4. Log in Command Center (failed batch = learning) 5. Update process doc

    Grants & Funding¶

    Q: Where do I find grants?¶

    A: - Foundation Directory Online (Candid) - GrantWatch, GrantStation - Local community foundation - Corporate foundations (local HQ) - DonorsChoose (crowdfunding for teachers) - State Dept of Education (STEM grants)

    Q: What makes a strong application?¶

    A: - Narrative: "Student-run circular manufacturing diverting 10K caps/yr" - Metrics: Baseline → Target (caps, kg, CO2e, students, revenue) - Sustainability: Revenue covers consumables Year 2+ - Scalability: Open-source, district replication plan - Student voice: Testimonials, photos, data

    Q: Can product revenue fund the program?¶

    A: Yes! Typical Year 1: - $500-2,000 revenue - Covers: parchment, molds, PPE, filament - Reinvest: new molds, equipment upgrades, garden - Key: Reinvest 100% → compounding growth

    Troubleshooting Quick Reference¶

    Symptom Likely Cause Quick Fix
    Sheets warp Cool time / weight 10 min + steel plate
    Burn marks Temp / time / parchment -5°C, -1 min, fresh paper
    Uneven melt Flake size / spread Sieve 5-8mm, spread even
    Shredder jam Feed rate / debris Reverse→Stop→Stick
    Sensor offline WiFi / webhook / power Reboot, check creds
    No data in dashboard Sheets not published Publish to web → CSV
    Low sales Price / visibility Promo, bundle, relocate
    Contamination Liners / dirt Longer soak, better sort
    Warped mold Cool too fast / no vent Slow cool, add vents
    Flash on parts Pressure high / temp high Lower pressure/temp

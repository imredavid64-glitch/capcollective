    HANDOUT 2: "CAP'S JOURNEY" STORY (K-2 read-aloud)¶

    CAP'S JOURNEY

Hi! I'm Cap. I came on top of a water bottle.
Someone drank the water. Then... where do I go?

Some caps fall on the ground.
Some caps float to the ocean. 🐟 Fish think I'm food. Oh no!

But NOT me! I went into the CAP BOX. ♻️

My friends and I were washed and dried.
We got chopped into tiny confetti! ✨
Then we got pressed flat like a pancake. 🥞
Now I'm a COASTER! Someone puts their drink on me every day.

I didn't become trash.
I became something useful.
I am part of the CIRCLE. 🔄

THE END

    HANDOUT 3: PARENT NEWSLETTER TEMPLATE¶

    [School Name] is launching CAP COLLECTIVE —
a student-run recycling program!

WHAT: Students collect bottle caps and turn them
      into coasters, organizers, and keys!
WHY:  Only 3% of caps get recycled. We're fixing that.
HOW:  Encourage your child to bring clean caps (#2/#5)
      to school. Drop-off boxes are at the cafeteria.

HOW YOU CAN HELP:
• Send clean caps with your child
• Volunteer for a collection day
• Visit our Eco-Store to buy student-made products

Questions? Contact [email].

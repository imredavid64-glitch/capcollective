# Cap Collective — Procurement, Payment & Finance Playbook
### How Money Actually Moves: Requisitions, POs, Invoices, Grants, Vendors & Receiving
**For the Program Lead & Finance Office | The "how do I actually buy all this?" guide**

---

## PART 1: THE MONEY FLOW (Pick Your Path)

There are **5 ways** a school can pay for Cap Collective. Most schools use a mix.

| Path | Speed | Effort | Best For |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **A. School Budget → PO** | 2-6 wks | Medium | Reliable, most common |
| **B. PTA / Booster funds** | 2-4 wks | Low | Fast startup |
| **C. Grant** | 2-6 mo | High | Equipment-heavy, PR |
| **D. Donation / Sponsorship** | 2-8 wks | Medium | Community ties |
| **E. Revenue pre-sales** | 4-6 wks | Low | Kickstart, low capital |

> **Strategy:** Ask for **Path A or B for $500-1,000 now**, and run **Path C (grants) in parallel** to fund the scale-up.

## PART 2: PATH A — SCHOOL BUDGET & PURCHASE ORDER (The Standard Route)

### Step-by-step
```
1. Get quotes (2-3 vendors per item)
2. Write the budget justification (below)
3. Submit a PURCHASE REQUISITION (form) to finance
4. Finance raises a PURCHASE ORDER (PO)
5. PO goes to the vendor
6. Vendor delivers to school (quote the PO number)
7. School receives + inspects (receiving form)
8. Finance pays the INVOICE (you don't; the school does)
9. File all documents
```
**You never pay personally.** The school pays the vendor; you manage paperwork.

### Budget Justification (1-page, for finance)
```
CAP COLLECTIVE — BUDGET JUSTIFICATION

Requested: $1,120 (Core tier startup)
Budget code: [ask finance]
Alignment: [STEM / Sustainability / Curriculum]

Items:
  Heat press ...................... $350
  Hand tools & PPE ................ $250
  Collection box materials ........ $150
  Shredder ........................ $220
  Sensor kits (ESP32+IR ×5) ....... $50
  Consumables ..................... $100

Rationale: One-time startup. Product revenue (~$500/semester)
offsets recurring costs. Reduces waste-management spend by ~[X].

Expected outcomes: 10,000+ caps diverted, 50+ students certified,
$500+ revenue, curriculum for [X] students.
```
*(Itemized costs auto-generate from Command Center → Procurement tab.)*

### Purchase Requisition (what finance needs)
See **Forms Pack (`15_...`)**: Purchase Requisition Form. Fields usually required: item, qty, unit cost, vendor, total, budget code, justification, approvals.

## PART 3: THE PROCUREMENT PROCESS IN DETAIL

### 3.1 Getting Quotes (Required in most districts)
**Rule:** Many districts require **2-3 written quotes** above a threshold (e.g., $500 or $5,000).

**How:** Email each vendor the Quote Request Template (Forms Pack). Ask for:
- Unit price (educational pricing)
- VAT/tax handling
- Shipping cost & lead time
- Warranty terms
- Valid-until date

**SAMPLE QUOTE REQUEST EMAIL**
```
Subject: Quote request — educational equipment (school)

Dear [Vendor],

We are a school ([name], [country]) establishing a plastic
recycling/STEM workshop. Please provide an educational quote for:

1. [Heat press: specify model, 38x38cm platen] x1
2. [Shredder: specify spec] x1
3. [Hand tools + PPE bundle] x1

Please include: unit prices, VAT/tax treatment (we are
[VAT-exempt/public]), shipping, lead time, and warranty.

We may issue a school purchase order. Please confirm you accept POs.

Thank you,
[Name, role, school, address]
```

### 3.2 The Equipment Order Checklist
Before ordering, confirm:
- [ ] Tier selected (Core / FabLab / Center)
- [ ] Quotes collected (2-3)
- [ ] Budget approved + code known
- [ ] PO raised (if required)
- [ ] Shipping address = school (not home!)
- [ ] Delivery contact + access arranged
- [ ] Electrical compatibility confirmed (voltage/plug!)
- [ ] Ventilation ready before equipment arrives

### 3.3 Electrical Compatibility (Common Failure Point)
| Region | Voltage | Plug |
|--------|---------|------|
| EU | 230V / 50Hz | C/F |
| UK | 230V / 50Hz | G |
| US | 120V / 60Hz | A/B |
| US (heavy) | 240V | NEMA 6-15/6-20 |

**Check:** Heat presses/shredders must match local voltage. A US-purchased press may not work in the EU without a step-up transformer (which may exceed circuit limits). **Order from a regional supplier.**

## PART 4: PATH B — PTA / BOOSTER FUNDS

**Fastest cash.** PTA often holds discretionary funds.

**Ask (at a PTA meeting):**
> "Can the PTA fund a $[X] startup for a student recycling program that turns waste into products and funds the garden? One-time cost, and the students will present results at term end."

**Deliverables to PTA:** budget sheet, impact promise, term-end report.
**Payment:** PTA reimburses the school or pays vendor directly; keep receipts/submit invoices per their process.

## PART 5: PATH C — GRANTS (Long game)

### Grant lifecycle
```
Identify → Write → Submit → Wait → Award → Disburse → Spend → Report
   1wk      2-3wk   1d     1-6mo   done   net30-90   spend   qtrly
```
### Disbursement mechanics (how you GET the money)
| Method | How it works | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| **Reimbursement** | You spend, submit receipts, they pay school | You must front/carry cost or use school funds |
| **Advance/direct payment** | Funder pays school or vendor up front | Best — no cash flow issue |
| **Restricted fund** | Money into a school account code | Spend only per budget |
| **In-kind** | Funder donates equipment directly | No cash, but no flexibility |

**Ask the funder:** "Do you disburse as advance or reimbursement? Do you pay vendors directly?"

### Grant spending rules (don't break these)
- Spend only on **approved budget lines**
- Keep **every receipt** (scan + file by budget line)
- Meet **deadlines** (unspent funds may be returned)
- Submit **impact reports** on schedule → needed for follow-on grants

### Grant financial tracking
Use Command Center → **Budget & Finance** tab: tag each expense with the grant code; filter for reports.

---

## PART 6: PATH D — DONATIONS & SPONSORSHIP

**In-kind donations** (tools, materials, caps from cafés) — get a **donation receipt/in-kind form** for the donor (tax benefit) and log the fair value.

**Cash sponsorship:** use Corporate Sponsorship tiers (Grant file, Template 3). Confirm school's policy on accepting business donations.

---

## PART 7: PATH E — REVENUE PRE-SALES

Sell products/pledges before you have everything:
1. Design a simple catalog (Product Dev file).
2. Take pre-orders (order form + payment via school store/PTA).
3. Use the cash to buy consumables.
**Note:** Don't promise products you can't make — start with pre-made samples.

## PART 9: RECEIVING, INSPECTION & RETURNS

### Delivery & Receiving Checklist
```
☐ Confirm PO number on the packing slip
☐ Count boxes vs delivery note
☐ Inspect for damage (photograph ANY damage BEFORE signing)
☐ Check voltage/plug once opened
☐ Test each machine (power on per SOP)
☐ Record serial numbers (warranty!)
☐ Store manuals in the Program Binder
☐ Log receipt in Command Center → Procurement (status = Received)
☐ File packing slips with the PO
```

### If something is wrong
| Problem | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Damaged in transit | Photograph, note on delivery slip, contact vendor within 48h |
| Wrong item | Contact vendor; do not use; arrange return |
| DOA (dead on arrival) | Warranty claim — keep packaging |
| Missing parts | Report to vendor; note on slip |

## PART 10: CUSTOMS, VAT & TAX (Imports)
- **Public schools are often VAT-exempt** — confirm and state it on the PO.
- **Cross-border orders** may incur customs/import duty — ask the vendor if DDP (delivered duty paid) or ask who pays.
- **Keep the commercial invoice** for customs and for the finance file.
- **Educational discount keywords:** "Schulrabatt" (DE), "Remise Éducative" (FR), "Descuento educativo" (ES), "Educational pricing / VAT-exempt" (universal).

## PART 11: EXPENSE REPORTING & RECEIPT DISCIPLINE
Every purchase (even $5 of parchment) needs:
- Original receipt/invoice
- Budget line tag (equipment / consumable / maintenance / marketing)
- Date + who bought it + purpose

**Use Command Center → Budget & Finance** to log. Reconcile monthly with finance.

**Reimbursement form:** Forms Pack.

## PART 12: THE PAYMENT TIMELINE (Realistic)
| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| -4 | Quotes requested |
| -3 | Quotes received; budget justified |
| -2 | Requisition submitted |
| -1 | PO raised + sent to vendor |
| 0 | Order confirmed; lead time starts |
| +1-6 | Delivery (depends on item) |
| +6 | Received, inspected, signed off |
| +6-10 | Invoice paid by finance |
| +7 | Equipment in use |

**Reality check:** Allow **4-8 weeks** from approval to equipment-in-hand. Plan launch accordingly.

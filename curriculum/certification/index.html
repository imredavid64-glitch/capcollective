    Cap Collective — Certification & Training Pack¶

    Everything Needed to Safely Certify a Student Operator¶

    The kit references "10-question quiz" and "certification cards" — here they are, fully written.

    PART 1: TRAINING PATH (Recap & Detail)¶

    MODULE 0: Orientation (all)        20 min
MODULE 1: General Safety (all)     30 min
MODULE 2: Material ID (all)        20 min
MODULE 3: Machine-Specific (per)   30 min each
MODULE 4: Supervised Practice       30 min per machine
ASSESSMENT: Quiz (80%) + Practical
AWARD: Certification Card

    Trainer: Certified staff. Ratio: 1 trainer : 6 students max.

    PART 2: MODULE SCRIPTS (For the Trainer to Deliver)¶

    MODULE 0 — ORIENTATION (20 min)¶

    Objectives: Understand what the program does; know the 5 steps; know the rules exist for a reason. Deliver: 1. Why caps matter (3 min) — the marine debris story. 2. The 5 steps (5 min) — Collect → Clean → Shred → Melt → Refine. 3. Tour of stations (7 min) — point out each machine, PPE location, extinguisher, first aid, exits. 4. The rules (5 min) — no cert = no machine; PPE always; never exceed 200°C; report everything. Check: Each student can name the 5 steps and locate the extinguisher.

    MODULE 1 — GENERAL SAFETY (30 min)¶

    Objectives: Identify hazards; don correct PPE; know emergency procedures. Deliver: 1. PPE fitting (10 min) — goggles, gloves, respirator, apron (fit check each student). 2. Hazards walk (10 min) — point out hot surfaces, blades, cables, water/electrical separation. 3. Emergency drill (10 min) — fire, burn, cut, fume. Practice "Stop, Tell, Help." Check: Student states the response to a burn and where the fire blanket is.

    MODULE 2 — MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION (20 min)¶

    Objectives: Distinguish HDPE (#2) and PP (#5) from PET (#1) and PVC (#3); remove liners. Deliver: 1. Show real caps and the number-in-triangle symbol. 2. Sorting drill — mix caps, students sort into HDPE / PP / reject. 3. Liner removal demo — the silicone/foam inner seal. 4. Why it matters — mixed polymer = weak product; PVC = toxic gas. Check: Student sorts 10 caps correctly (≥9/10).

    MODULE 3 — MACHINE-SPECIFIC (30 min each)¶

    Deliver per machine following its SOP (Safety file Section 2): - Heat Press (Grade 6+, Red card) - Shredder (Grade 6+, Orange card) - Laser (Grade 8+, Green card) - Injection Molder (Grade 9+, Blue card) - Saws/Sander (Grade 9+, Purple card)

    MODULE 4 — SUPERVISED PRACTICE (30 min per machine)¶

    Student performs the full operation twice under supervision; trainer signs off on each step.

    PART 3: THE CERTIFICATION QUIZ¶

    QUIZ A — GENERAL SAFETY (Required for all cards)¶

    Name: __________________ Date: ______ Grade: ____ Pass mark: 8/10

    1. What is the ABSOLUTE maximum temperature any machine may reach? a) 140°C b) 180°C c) 200°C d) 260°C

    2. Which PPE is required at the heat press? (circle all) Goggles / Heat gloves / VOC respirator / Apron / Ear protection

    3. True or False: You may operate a machine if a friend supervises you.

    4. What do you do FIRST if you smell burning plastic?

    5. Which plastic is NEVER allowed (releases toxic gas)? a) HDPE #2 b) PP #5 c) PVC #3 d) LDPE #4

    6. Why must silicone/foam liners be removed from caps?

    7. If a cap touches the hot metal platen, what could happen?

    8. List the 5 steps of the process in order: 1) ______ 2) ______ 3) ______ 4) ______ 5) ______

    9. Where is the fire extinguisher / fire blanket located?

    10. True or False: You should report a near-miss even if no one was hurt.

    Quiz A Answer Key: 1-c; 2-all four (goggles, gloves, respirator, apron — not ear pro); 3-False; 4-Shut off machine / evacuate / ventilate / tell adult; 5-c; 6-They don't melt and ruin the product/contaminate it; 7-It burns/scorches, discolors and weakens the product; 8-Collect, Clean, Shred, Melt, Refine; 9-[school-specific]; 10-True.

    QUIZ B — HEAT PRESS (Red card)¶

    Pass mark: 8/10

    1. Correct temperature for HDPE? ____ For PP? ____ 2. What goes between the flakes and the metal platen? a) nothing b) parchment paper c) aluminum foil d) cloth 3. How long do you press before flipping? ____ 4. True/False: You may leave the press unattended while pressing. 5. What is "thermal runaway" and what do you do?

    6. How do you verify the platen is at temperature? ______________ 7. Why cool the sheet "under weight"? ______________ 8. What PPE is mandatory? ______________ 9. Target sheet thickness tolerance? ______ 10. What do you do with a failed (burned) sheet? ______________

    Answer Key B: 1-140°C / 170°C; 2-b; 3-5 min; 4-False; 5-Temp keeps rising beyond set point — cut power at wall, evacuate, report; 6-IR thermometer; 7-Prevent warping; 8-Goggles, heat gloves, respirator, apron; 9-±0.5mm; 10-Re-shred and reuse (never bin the plastic).

    QUIZ C — SHREDDER (Orange card)¶

    Pass mark: 8/10

    1. How do you push material into the shredder? ______ 2. How fast do you feed caps? ______ 3. What do you do if the motor stalls? ______ 4. Target flake size? ______ 5. True/False: You may reach into the hopper to clear a jam while it's running. 6. What PPE is required? ______ 7. What is the e-stop and where is it? ______ 8. Why sieve the flakes? ______ 9. What maintenance happens after each session? ______ 10. Who may operate the shredder? ______

    Answer Key C: 1-With the plunger tool, never hands; 2-Slowly, a few at a time; 3-Stop, reverse, clear, restart; 4-5-8mm; 5-False; 6-Goggles, ear protection, apron; 7-Emergency stop button — [location]; 8-Consistent size = better melt; 9-Oil the blades; 10-Only certified operators (Orange card, Grade 6+).

    QUIZ D — LASER (Green card) — abbreviated¶

    1. Focus the beam on the ____ of the material. (surface)
    2. For recycled plastic use ____ power and ____ speed. (low / high)
    3. Ventilation must be ____ while cutting. (on)
    4. Never cut ____ plastic (releases chlorine gas). (PVC)
    5. True/False: You may leave the laser cutting and walk away. (False)
    6. Keep a ____ within reach at all times. (fire extinguisher)
    7. If the material ignites, ____ (pause/stop, don't open lid immediately, follow fire SOP).
    8. Minimum grade for laser certification? (Grade 8)
    9. What do you do before cutting a new material? (confirm it's approved plastic)
    10. Where do you record the cut? (Command Center batch log)

    QUIZ E — INJECTION MOLDER (Blue card) — abbreviated¶

    1. Barrel temperature for HDPE. (~180°C)
    2. What secures the mold under the nozzle? (C-clamp, tight)
    3. How long to wait before demolding? (2-3 min)
    4. How is plastic injected? (steady firm lever pressure until stop)
    5. What is the gate / vent for? (plastic entry / air escape)
    6. Why apply release agent? (so product doesn't stick)
    7. Minimum grade? (Grade 9)
    8. What PPE? (goggles, heat gloves, respirator, apron)
    9. Never touch the ____. (barrel/nozzle)
    10. Failed part → ? (re-shred, mono-material)

    QUIZ F — SAW / SANDER (Purple card) — abbreviated¶

    1. Keep fingers at least ____ from the blade. (5 cm)
    2. Feed material ____ the blade rotation. (against)
    3. Tie back ____; remove ____. (hair / jewelry)
    4. Clamp the work — True/False? (True — always clamp)
    5. Where does dust go? (extraction/capture)
    6. PPE? (goggles, ear protection, apron, dust mask)
    7. What before changing a blade? (disconnect power)
    8. Never reach ____ a moving blade. (across)
    9. Minimum grade? (Grade 9)
    10. After use: (wait for full stop, clear dust)

    PART 6: VIDEO SCRIPT OUTLINES (For "substitute-friendly" demos)¶

    Record these once; reuse forever. Host on the school's private channel.

    Video Length Script outline
    V1 "Welcome to Cap Collective" 3 min Why caps, the 5 steps, the rules, tour
    V2 "Material ID" 3 min Number-in-triangle, sort demo, liner removal
    V3 "Wash Station" 3 min Soak, scrub, rinse, dry 24h
    V4 "Shredder" 4 min PPE, pre-check, feed slowly, e-stop, oil
    V5 "Heat Press" 5 min Temp set, parchment sandwich, 5+5 min, cool
    V6 "Sensor & Dashboard" 4 min How counting works, how to read the dashboard
    V7 "Emergency Procedures" 3 min Fire, burn, cut, fume — what to do

    Script format for each: (a) hook, (b) PPE, (c) step-by-step demo with captions, (d) common mistake, (e) recap + "get certified to do this yourself."

    PART 8: RECERTIFICATION & REFRESH¶

    • Every 6 months, or
    • Start of a new school year, or
    • After any safety incident, or
    • After a long break (>8 weeks no operation). Refresh = re-watch video + re-pass quiz + quick practical re-check.

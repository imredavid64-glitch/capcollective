    W-K2 · CAP'S JOURNEY (K-2)¶

    Name: ___________________ Date: __________

    Number the steps 1-5 to show the GOOD journey. Draw arrows. 

    [ ] Bottle cap on a bottle
[ ] Cap goes in the Cap Collective box
[ ] Caps are washed
[ ] Caps are melted
[ ] A new coaster is made!
    Which is better — the ocean or the box? Draw a circle around it. Why?

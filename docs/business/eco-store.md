# Cap Collective — Eco-Store Operations Manual
### How to Actually Sell Student-Made Products (and Stay Compliant)
**Covers pricing, point-of-sale, inventory, events, online sales, and money handling.**

---

# PART 1: STORE CONCEPT & POLICY

**What it is:** A student-run pop-up shop selling recycled-cap products (coasters, organizers, keychains, keychains, pots). Revenue funds the garden and materials.

**Two operating models — pick one:**
| Model | Best for | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Pop-up shop** | Most schools | Open during lunch/events only; low overhead |
| **Online store** | Larger programs | Etsy/Square/Shopify; needs adult account-holder |

> **Policy check:** Confirm with the business office whether the school can accept revenue, or whether sales must route through a booster/PTA 501(c)(3). **Never** run sales through a personal account.

# PART 2: PRICING STRATEGY

## The Formula
```
PRICE = (MATERIAL + OVERHEAD + LABOR) × MARKUP

Material: caps (free) + color, hardware, packaging
Overhead: electricity, wear, consumables (allocate ~10%)
Labor: student time × $0 (educational) OR small stipend
Markup: 2.5-3× for retail
```

## Suggested Price List
| Product | Material cost | Suggested price | Margin |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|--------|
| Coaster (single) | $0.80 | $10 | $9.20 |
| Coaster set (4) | $3.00 | $30 | $27 |
| Keychain | $0.30 | $4 | $3.70 |
| Phone stand | $1.20 | $15 | $13.80 |
| Plant pot | $1.50 | $18 | $16.50 |
| Desk organizer | $2.50 | $25 | $22.50 |
| Cap art tile | $1.00 | $12 | $11 |

**Bundles:** "Garden Starter Kit" (2 pots + 1 coaster) — $40 (save $6).
**Bundle value** = higher average sale.

# PART 3: POINT OF SALE (POS)

## No-Budget Option (Cash Box)
```
Supplies: cash box with lock, receipt book, price signs, change float ($50)
Procedure:
1. Two students staff the store (never one alone).
2. Record every sale in the Sales Log (Command Center).
3. Count cash at open and close with an adult present.
4. Adult holds the cash; deposit weekly per school policy.
```

## Low-Cost Digital Option
- **Square Reader** (~$10 + 2.6% fee) — tap/insert cards, works on phone.
- **Venmo/Zelle for parents** — route to the adult/PTA account only.
- **QR to an order form** — good for pre-orders/events.

## Sale Log Format
```
Date | Product | Qty | Unit price | Total | Payment (cash/card) | Staff initials
-----|---------|-----|-----------|-------|--------------------|----------------
```

# PART 4: INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

## Tracking
- Every finished product gets an ID + entry in **Product Inventory** (Command Center tab 04 / `csv/04_Product_Inventory.csv`).
- Log: product, batch #, date made, cost, price, status (in stock / sold / gifted / donated).

## Stock Rules
- **Min stock:** keep ≥5 of best-sellers.
- **Max stock:** don't overproduce slow movers — cap at 20.
- **FIFO:** sell oldest stock first (color may fade).
- **Reserved:** hold 10% for donors/partners/prizes.

## Reorder Point Formula
```
Reorder when: Current stock ≤ (average weekly sales × lead time) + safety stock
Example: weekly sales 6, lead time 1 week, safety 4 → reorder at ≤10
```

# PART 6: ONLINE STORE (Optional, Larger Programs)

## Platform Comparison
| Platform | Fee | Best for |
|----------|-----|----------|
| Square Online | 2.6%+30¢ | Simple, card-native |
| Etsy | 6.5% + fees | Built-in audience |
| Shopify | Monthly fee | Serious scale |
| School store page | Free | Lowest cost |

## Rules
- Billing details must be the school/PTA — **never a student's**.
- Shipping: padded envelope, $5 flat (US).
- Photos: plain background, natural light, 3 angles.
- Turnaround: state clearly ("made to order, ships in 5 days").

# PART 7: MONEY HANDLING & CONTROLS

**Two-person rule:** Every cash count has two people (one adult).
**Segregation:** The person who sells ≠ the person who deposits.
**Weekly deposit:** Per school finance policy.
**Ledger:** Record every dollar in and out in the **Impact Dashboard** finance tab.

## Simple P&L (Monthly)
```
REVENUE                    $______
Materials & consumables    -$______
Packaging                  -$______
Fees (cards)               -$______
Event supplies             -$______
NET                        $______
```

> **Educational note:** It's fine to reinvest 100% into the program. State this publicly — donors love it.

---

# PART 8: COMPLIANCE & ETHICS
- Follow the school's **fundraising policy** (some require board approval).
- Never use student photos in marketing without a signed **photo release** (`15_FORMS_...`).
- Label products honestly: "Made from recycled HDPE #2 collected at [School]."
- Do not claim food-safety for items touching food/drink unless verified — sell **coasters/organizers**, not cups/plates, unless you clear it.
- Comply with any **sales-tax** rules (schools often exempt — confirm).

# Cap Collective — Safety SOPs & Risk Assessment
### Standard Operating Procedures for All Machines & Processes
**Version 1.0 | Mandatory reading before any student machine access**

---

## SECTION 1: GENERAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 The Golden Rules
1. **No certification = no machine access.** Ever.
2. **One certified adult per 4 students** at any powered machine.
3. **Never exceed 200°C** on any heating device.
4. **Ventilation always on** before heating any plastic.
5. **PPE at all times** at machine stations.
6. **Stop work immediately** if you smell burning, see smoke, or hear grinding.
7. **Hands stay out** of shredder hopper and press platen at all times.

### 1.2 Required PPE by Station
| Station | Goggles | Heat Gloves | Respiration | Apron | Ear Pro |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|-------|---------|
| Collection | — | — | — | — | — |
| Wash | ☐ | — | — | ☐ | — |
| Shredder | ☐ | — | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| Heat Press | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | — |
| Injection Molder | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | — |
| Laser | ☐ | — | ☐ | — | — |
| Sander/Saw | ☐ | — | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

### 1.3 Emergency Procedures
**FIRE:** Activate fire blanket/CO₂ extinguisher. Do NOT use water on plastic fires. Evacuate. Call emergency services.
**BURN:** Cool with running water 10+ min. Do not pop blisters. Report to first aider.
**CUT:** Apply pressure, elevate, seek first aid. Deep cuts → emergency services.
**FUME/HEADACHE:** Turn off machine, evacuate room, ventilate 15 min, report.
**FULL COLLECTION BOX:** Safe — swap box, do not compress.

### 1.4 Incident Reporting
Every incident (near-miss or actual) logged within 24 hours using the form in Appendix. Program Lead reviews weekly.

## SECTION 2: MACHINE SOPs

### 2.1 HEAT PRESS SOP
```
▛▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▜
▌  HEAT PRESS — OPERATOR PROCEDURE                    ▐
▟▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▟
```
**BEFORE (each session):**
1. Confirm ventilation is ON (feel airflow, check fan running)
2. Don goggles, gloves, respirator, apron
3. Check cord, plug, no damage
4. Verify IR thermometer reads room temp

**STARTUP:**
1. Press red I/O button (ON)
2. Press SET → set temperature (HDPE 140°C / PP 170°C)
3. Press SET → set time (5:00)
4. Wait for FULL temp — verify with IR thermometer

**OPERATION:**
1. Place parchment sheet on platen
2. Arrange flakes (evenly, ~equal coverage)
3. Cover with second parchment sheet
4. Lower platen (do not touch hot surface)
5. Maintain pressure 5 minutes
6. Release, flip the "sandwich", press 5 minutes again
7. Cool under weight 5 minutes
8. Remove, inspect

**⚠️ DANGER — THERMAL RUNAWAY:** If temp exceeds set point and keeps rising → cut power at wall → evacuate → report.

**SHUTDOWN:** Off → cool 20 min → clean platen with approved cleaner.

**Maintenance:** Clean platen weekly. Check cable monthly.

### 2.2 SHREDDER SOP
```
▛▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▀▜
▌  SHREDDER — OPERATOR PROCEDURE                      ▐
▟▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▟
```
**BEFORE:**
1. Don goggles, ear protection, apron
2. Clear blades of residual plastic
3. Check emergency stop button works
4. Verify catch bin is empty

**OPERATION:**
1. Toggle motor — verify correct rotation
2. **Feed slowly** — a few caps at a time
3. NEVER use hands to push material — use the plunger
4. If motor stalls → stop → reverse → clear → restart
5. Target flake size: 5-8mm
6. Sieve through mesh screen

**⚠️ NEVER:** Put hands in hopper, overfeed, run unattended, remove guards.

**SHUTDOWN:** Run until clear → off → clean → oil blades.

---

### 2.3 INJECTION MOLDER SOP (Center tier, 9-12 only)
**BEFORE:** Full PPE. Verify ventilation. Inspect mold.
**STARTUP:** Set barrel to 180°C. Wait for full heat.
**OPERATION:**
1. Load flakes into hopper
2. Wait for molten state
3. Clamp mold tightly (C-clamp)
4. Pull lever — steady, firm pressure until stop
5. Wait 2-3 min for solidification
6. Demold carefully (use pliers)
**⚠️ NEVER:** Touch barrel. Open mold while hot. Force jammed plunger.
**SHUTDOWN:** Purge barrel. Off. Cool.

---

### 2.4 LASER CUTTER SOP (Center tier)
**BEFORE:** Extract ventilation ON. Fire extinguisher within reach. Know material (recycled plastic only — no PVC!).
**OPERATION:**
1. Focus beam on sheet surface
2. Set LOW power / HIGH speed for recycled plastic (prevents flaring)
3. **NEVER leave laser unattended while cutting**
4. Watch for flame; pause if ignited
**⚠️ NEVER:** Cut unknown plastics (PVC releases chlorine gas). Run unattended.
**SHUTDOWN:** Off → clean lens → clear debris.

---

### 2.5 SANDER / FRET SAW / BANDSAW SOP
**BEFORE:** Goggles, ear pro, apron. Tie back hair. Remove jewelry.
**OPERATION:** Clamp work. Keep fingers 5cm+ from blade. Feed against rotation. Never reach across.
**SHUTDOWN:** Off. Wait for full stop. Clear dust.

## SECTION 3: MATERIAL SAFETY

### 3.1 Approved Materials
| Material | ID | Melt Temp | Approved? |
|----------|-----|-----------|-----------|
| HDPE | #2 | 130°C | ✅ YES (primary) |
| PP | #5 | 160°C | ✅ YES |
| LDPE | #4 | 110°C | ✅ YES (advanced) |
| PET | #1 | 260°C | ⚠️ Advanced only |
| PVC | #3 | — | ❌ NEVER (toxic fumes) |
| PS | #6 | — | ❌ Avoid |
| PLA (3D) | — | 180°C | ✅ For filament only |

### 3.2 Cleaning Checklist (Prevents Toxic Fumes)
- [ ] Remove all silicone/foam liners
- [ ] Wash in warm soapy water
- [ ] Remove all labels & adhesive
- [ ] Rinse until clear
- [ ] Air dry 24 hrs
- [ ] Reject any cap with residual smell

### 3.3 Temperature Limits
| Threshold | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| HDPE 140°C | Normal |
| PP 170°C | Normal |
| **200°C** | **ABSOLUTE MAX — never exceed** |
| >200°C | Shut down, ventilate, report |

## SECTION 4: RISK ASSESSMENT MATRIX

### 4.1 Completed Risk Register
| Ref | Hazard | Who | L | S | Risk | Controls | Residual |
|-----|--------|-----|---|---|------|----------|----------|
| R1 | Burns from press/molder | Students | Med | High | **HIGH** | Gloves, temp monitor, no-touch rule, supervision, IR thermo | Low |
| R2 | Toxic fumes from overheating | All | Low | High | **MED** | Ventilation, temp limits, clean material, respirators | Low |
| R3 | Cuts from shredder blades | Students | Med | Med | **MED** | Guards, plunger tool, training, first aid | Low |
| R4 | Cuts from saw/sander/laser | Students | Med | Med | **MED** | Guards, clamps, training, PPE | Low |
| R5 | Electrical shock | All | Low | High | **MED** | RCD/GFCI, 16A circuit, no water near machines, inspection | Low |
| R6 | Fire | All | Low | High | **MED** | Extinguisher, blanket, temp limits, no unattended laser | Low |
| R7 | Eye injury (fragments) | Students | Med | Med | **MED** | Goggles mandatory, guards | Low |
| R8 | Slips on wet floor (wash) | Students | Med | Low | **LOW** | Non-slip mats, mop immediately, signage | Low |
| R9 | Inhalation of microplastics | Students | Low | Med | **LOW** | Dust extraction at sander, masks | Low |
| R10 | Allergic reaction to cleaners | Students | Low | Med | **LOW** | Mild soap only, gloves, MSDS on file | Low |

**L = Likelihood, S = Severity. Review termly.**

### 4.2 Control Hierarchy Applied
1. **Elimination:** Only safe materials (no PVC).
2. **Substitution:** Lower temps, mild cleaners.
3. **Engineering:** Guards, ventilation, RCD, emergency stops.
4. **Administrative:** Certification, supervision, SOPs, rotation limits.
5. **PPE:** Last line of defense.

## SECTION 5: TRAINING & CERTIFICATION

### 5.1 Certification Path
```
Step 1: Watch safety video (20 min)
Step 2: Read this SOP for target machine
Step 3: Pass 10-question quiz (80%)
Step 4: Supervised practical demo (30 min)
Step 5: Sign Student Safety Contract
Step 6: Receive color-coded Certification Card
```

### 5.3 Recertification
Every 6 months, or after any safety incident, or start of new school year.

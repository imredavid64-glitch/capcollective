# Grade-Band Programs

Cap Collective scales with students from kindergarten through high school.

---

## K-2: Cap Detectives
**Focus:** Observation, sorting, storytelling

| Activity | Standards | Time |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Cap hunt & color sort | K-ESS3-3, 2-PS1-1 | 30 min |
| "Cap's Journey" story | ELA.RL.K.2 | 20 min |
| Decorate pre-made coasters | VA:Cr1.1.K | 45 min |
| Picture-based data entry | MP.4, MP.5 | 15 min |

**Products:** Decorated coasters only (no machines)
**Tech:** Tablet for teacher data entry
**Outcome:** "I can identify HDPE!" badge

---

## 3-5: Cap Engineers
**Focus:** Measurement, building, basic design

| Activity | Standards | Time |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Build collection boxes | 3-5-ETS1-1 | 90 min |
| Wash → shred (supervised) → press | 5-PS1-3 | 60 min |
| Tinkercad coaster design | 3-5-ETS1-2 | 60 min |
| Weigh, graph, calculate yield | 5.MD.B.2 | 30 min |

**Products:** Coasters, simple injection keychains
**Tech:** Google Forms, simple charts, Tinkercad
**Outcome:** "Cap Engineer" certificate

---

## 6-8: Cap Innovators
**Focus:** Data analysis, product development, entrepreneurship

| Activity | Standards | Time |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Program ESP32 IR counters | MS-ETS1-2 | 90 min |
| Build dashboard (Sheets/Looker) | MS-PS1-3 | 60 min |
| Fusion 360 mold design | MS-ETS1-3 | 90 min |
| Business plan & pricing | MS-ETS1-1 | 60 min |

**Products:** Full product line + custom orders
**Tech:** Arduino, Sheets/CSV analysis, 3D printing molds, laser cutting
**Outcome:** Product portfolio + sales revenue

---

## 9-12: Cap Systems Designers
**Focus:** Systems engineering, LCA, policy, scale

| Activity | Standards | Time |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) | HS-ETS1-3, HS-ESS3-4 | 90 min |
| R&D: new materials (PP, LDPE) | HS-PS2-6 | 90 min |
| Supply chain optimization | HS-ETS1-2 | 60 min |
| Grant writing & B2B sales | HS-ETS1-1 | 90 min |

**Products:** Architectural tiles, furniture components, B2B orders
**Tech:** Python analysis, API integration, CNC mold making, filament extrusion
**Outcome:** Professional portfolio, college-ready capstone

## Cross-Curricular Connections

| Subject | K-2 | 3-5 | 6-8 | 9-12 |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|------|
| **Science** | Materials, recycling | States of matter, pollution | Chemical recycling, LCA | Polymer chemistry, circular economy |
| **Math** | Counting, sorting, graphing | Measurement, fractions, area | Statistics, optimization | Calculus (extrusion rates), economics |
| **CS/Tech** | Tablet data entry | Google Forms, charts | Arduino, dashboards, Fusion 360 | Python, API, CNC, AI prediction |
| **Art/Design** | Decorate coasters | Tinkercad design | Product design, branding | Industrial design, UX |
| **Business** | Class store simulation | Pricing, profit/loss | Business plan, marketing | Supply chain, grants, B2B sales |

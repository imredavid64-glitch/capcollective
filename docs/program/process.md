# The 5-Step Process

Cap Collective transforms bottle caps into products through a safe, student-run micro-factory.

```
1. COLLECT  →  HDPE (#2) / PP (#5) caps from campus boxes
2. CLEAN    →  Remove liners, wash, air-dry 24 hrs
3. SHRED    →  5-8mm uniform flakes
4. MELT     →  Heat press: HDPE 140°C / PP 170°C (never >200°C)
5. REFINE   →  Laser cut / injection mold / 3D filament
```

**Closed loop:** Products are themselves recyclable → back to Step 3.

---

## Step Details

### 1. COLLECT
- Students deploy collection boxes in high-traffic zones (cafeteria, gym, entry, library)
- 1 box per 150 students minimum
- Optional: ESP32 + IR sensor for auto-counting
- Student "Green Team" collects Fridays, weighs, logs

### 2. CLEAN
- Remove all plastic liners (soak 10 min if stuck)
- Warm soapy water wash
- Air-dry on mesh racks 24 hours minimum
- **No towels** — lint contaminates flakes

### 3. SHRED
- Target flake size: 5-8mm (critical for quality)
- Sieve through mesh screen
- Discard fines (<5mm) — re-shred or compost
- Certified operators only (14+)

### 4. MELT (Heat Press)
| Material | Temperature | Time |
|----------|-------------|------|
| HDPE (#2) | 140°C | 5 min + flip + 5 min |
| PP (#5) | 170°C | 5 min + flip + 5 min |
| **Never exceed** | **200°C** | — |

- Parchment paper sandwich (top + bottom, mandatory)
- Cool under weight 10 minutes
- QC check: thickness ±0.5mm, no burns, smooth edges

### 5. REFINE
- **Laser cutter:** Coasters, tiles, organizers (Core/FabLab/Center)
- **Injection molder:** Keychains, badges, pots (FabLab/Center)
- **Filament extruder:** 3D printer filament (Center)
- Hand-cut with templates if no laser (Core)

---

## Safety Rules (Non-Negotiable)
- No certification = no machine access
- 1 certified adult per 4 students at powered machines
- Ventilation always ON before heating (100+ CFM)
- PPE: goggles, heat gloves (500°F), N95+ respirator, apron
- Stop immediately if burning smell, smoke, or grinding noise

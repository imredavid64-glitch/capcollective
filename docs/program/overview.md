# Cap Collective — Program Overview

> *"The distance between an idea and its impact is the bridge we build today."*

---

## What Is Cap Collective?

Cap Collective is a **student-led circular recycling program** that captures discarded plastic bottle caps and transforms them into useful products — while funding the school garden. Students run every part of it: collection, cleaning, shredding, melting, product design, sales, and impact reporting.

Bottle caps are a **recycling blind spot**. Mechanical recycling centers process large items like bottles, but caps are too small — they fall through sorting screens or jam high-speed equipment. The result: a perfectly good, high-grade plastic is thrown away, every day, in every school.

Cap Collective captures that resource **at the source** and turns it into value.

---

## The Problem in One Line

Only about **3% of bottle caps** are recycled globally. The rest end up in landfill or the ocean — where they look like food to sea turtles and fish.

---

## The Solution: A 5-Step Micro-Factory in Your School

```
1. COLLECT   →  HDPE (#2) & PP (#5) caps dropped in campus boxes
2. CLEAN     →  Remove liners · wash · air-dry 24 hours
3. SHRED     →  Uniform 5-8 mm flakes
4. MELT      →  Heat press: HDPE 140°C · PP 170°C  (never above 200°C)
5. REFINE    →  Laser-cut · injection-mold · press into finished goods
```

**Closed loop:** When a product breaks, it goes right back to Step 3. 100% recyclable, forever.

---

## Why Caps?

| Property | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **HDPE (#2) & PP (#5)** | Melt predictably; safe when heated carefully |
| **High density & color** | Strong products with beautiful marbled finishes |
| **Abundant** | Every school produces thousands per term |
| **Food-safe grade** | Suitable for useful everyday items |
| **Ignored by industry** | An untapped resource, not a nuisance |

No PVC (#3) is ever used — it releases toxic gas when heated.

## What Students Make

Coasters · keychains · phone stands · desk organizers · plant pots · art tiles · and more.

Each product uses a known number of caps (e.g., a keychain ≈ 4 caps, a coaster ≈ 15 caps), so students can see — and calculate — exactly how waste becomes value.

## Learn While You Build

Cap Collective is a **full curriculum platform**, not just a recycling bin. It spans four grade bands:

- **K-2 — Cap Detectives:** observation, sorting, storytelling
- **3-5 — Cap Engineers:** measurement, building, basic design
- **6-8 — Cap Innovators:** data, sensors, entrepreneurship
- **9-12 — Cap Systems Designers:** engineering, life-cycle analysis, policy, scale

Every lesson links to standards (NGSS, Common Core, IB) and comes with printable worksheets.

## Safety Is Non-Negotiable

- **No certification = no machine access.**
- Maximum temperature: **200°C** — never exceeded.
- Ventilation mandatory for all heating.
- PPE required per station.
- Every machine has a Standard Operating Procedure and a student certification card.

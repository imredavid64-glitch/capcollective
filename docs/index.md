# Cap Collective — School Implementation Kit

> **"The distance between an idea and its impact is the bridge we build today."**

Welcome to the **Cap Collective** documentation. This is a complete, open-source K-12 circular economy program where students collect HDPE bottle caps, process them into flakes, and manufacture valuable products — all while learning STEM, entrepreneurship, and environmental stewardship.

---

## 🎯 What Is Cap Collective?

Cap Collective is a **student-led circular recycling program** that captures discarded plastic bottle caps and transforms them into useful products (coasters, keychains, organizers, tiles) while funding school gardens and grants.

**The Problem:** Only ~3% of bottle caps are recycled globally. They're too small for industrial sorting machines — they fall through the cracks.

**The Solution:** Source separation at school + micro-manufacturing = high-value products + measurable impact + standards-aligned curriculum.

## 🛡️ Safety First

- **No certification = no machine access**
- **Max temperature: 200°C** (never exceeded)
- **Ventilation mandatory** (100+ CFM) for all heating
- **PPE required** per station (goggles, heat gloves, respirator, apron)
- **1 certified adult per 4 students** at machines

See [Safety Documentation](program/safety.md) for complete SOPs.

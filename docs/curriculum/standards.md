## 6. ASSESSMENT RUBRIC (Universal, 4-Point)

| Criterion | 4 — Exceeds | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approach. | 1 — Beginning |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Understanding** | Explains connections to broader systems | Explains core concept accurately | Partial understanding | Misconceptions present |
| **Process Skill** | Performs independently, teaches others | Performs with minimal help | Needs prompting | Cannot yet perform |
| **Data Use** | Draws insights, predicts | Records & graphs correctly | Records with errors | Incomplete data |
| **Safety** | Models best practice, corrects others | Follows all rules | Occasional reminders | Unsafe — needs close supervision |
| **Collaboration** | Leads, includes all | Works well in team | Participates unevenly | Disrupts/disengages |

**Use for:** Products (Design), Data reports (Math/CS), Presentations (ELA), Process (Science).

## 7. SAMPLE SUMMATIVE PERFORMANCE TASK
**"Design a Product Line Pitch" (Grades 6-12, 2 weeks)**
- **Task:** Teams design 2 products, build a prototype, cost it, and pitch to a panel.
- **Deliverables:** CAD files, physical prototype, cost sheet, 5-min pitch.
- **Rubric:** Product design (MYP Design C), Economics (Math/Business), Presentation (ELA).
- **Standards:** MS-ETS1-1/2, HS-ETS1-3, W.9-10.1, SL.9-10.4.

## 8. ADAPTING TO YOUR NATIONAL CURRICULUM
Not in the US/IB system? Use this mapping method:

1. **List your subjects** (Science, Math, Tech, Art, Civics).
2. **Find your standards** for materials, data, design, citizenship.
3. **Match Cap Collective activities** to each — nearly everything fits.
4. **Document** using the Curriculum Tracker tab in the Command Center.
5. **Keep evidence** (photos, sheets, products) for inspections/reviews.

**Universal fit:** Any curriculum with science, math, design/technology, or citizenship education can adopt this program.

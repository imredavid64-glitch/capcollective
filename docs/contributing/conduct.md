# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- **Empathy & Kindness:** Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people
- **Respect:** Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Constructive Feedback:** Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- **Accountability:** Accept responsibility and apologize to those affected by our mistakes, and learn from the experience
- **Community Focus:** Focus on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- **Inclusion:** Actively welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities
- **Mentorship:** Support newcomers, share knowledge generously, celebrate others' successes

### Unacceptable Behavior
- **Harassment:** Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- **Discrimination:** Demeaning comments based on protected characteristics
- **Sexual Harassment:** Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexually explicit content
- **Doxxing:** Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- **Threats:** Physical threats, violence, intimidation, stalking
- **Spam/Self-Promotion:** Off-topic marketing, excessive self-promotion
- **Disruption:** Deliberate disruption of discussions, events, or workflows
- **Impersonation:** Pretending to be another person or entity
- **Illegal Activity:** Encouraging or facilitating illegal acts

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces:
- GitHub (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
- Discord server
- Email lists
- Social media (when representing Cap Collective)
- In-person events (workshops, conferences, meetups)
- Virtual events (webinars, office hours, hackathons)
- Any other spaces managed by the Cap Collective community

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting
**Report violations to:** conduct@capcollective.org

**Include:**
- Your contact info (can be anonymous)
- Description of incident (what, when, where)
- People involved
- Any evidence (screenshots, logs, links)
- Desired outcome (if any)

**All reports are confidential.** We will not reveal reporter identity without explicit consent.

### Response Process
1. **Acknowledgment** within 24 hours
2. **Investigation** by Code of Conduct Committee (3 members)
3. **Decision** within 7 days (or communicated timeline)
4. **Action** implemented
5. **Follow-up** with reporter

### Enforcement Guidelines

| Severity | Behavior | Consequence |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **1. Correction** | Inappropriate language, minor disruption | Private written warning, public apology if public |
| **2. Warning** | Repeated minor, single moderate (insults, mild harassment) | Temporary ban (7-30 days), public if public |
| **3. Temporary Ban** | Severe harassment, discrimination, threats | 30-90 day ban from all spaces, appeal after 30 days |
| **4. Permanent Ban** | Severe/repeated violations, doxxing, violence, illegal acts | Permanent ban from all spaces, no appeal |

**Factors considered:** Impact (not intent), pattern, power dynamics, vulnerability of target, community safety.

## Community Guidelines by Platform

### GitHub
- **Issues:** Use templates, search first, one topic per issue
- **PRs:** Follow template, link issues, keep focused
- **Discussions:** Stay on topic, be constructive
- **Reviews:** Be kind, specific, actionable
- **Labels:** Use correctly, don't spam

### Discord
- **Channels:** Use correct channel, read pins
- **Voice:** Push-to-talk, mute when not speaking
- **Reactions:** Use for voting/acknowledging, not spam
- **DMs:** Don't DM without consent (except mods)
- **Roles:** Don't ping @everyone/@here without need

### Events (Virtual & In-Person)
- **Registration:** Required, includes CoC acceptance
- **Badges:** Visible, include pronouns (optional)
- **Harassment:** Report to event staff (identified) immediately
- **Alcohol:** If served, responsible consumption only
- **Photos:** Opt-out available (badge sticker)
- **Accessibility:** Quiet room, live captions, dietary needs

## Moderation Team

### Code of Conduct Committee
- 3 rotating members (1-year terms)
- Diverse representation (role, geography, identity)
- Trained in trauma-informed response
- Confidentiality agreements

### Moderators (Discord/GitHub)
- Enforce platform-specific guidelines
- Escalate to Committee for CoC violations
- Document actions in private mod log

### Event Safety Team
- Designated at each event (visible badges)
- Trained in bystander intervention
- Direct line to venue security/emergency services

---

## Appeals Process

**Appeal to:** appeals@capcollective.org (different from conduct@)

**Timeline:** Within 14 days of decision

**Process:**
1. Different Committee members review
2. Original evidence + new evidence
3. Decision within 14 days
3. Final (no further appeal)

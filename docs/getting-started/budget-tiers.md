# Budget Tiers

Choose the tier that fits your school's resources and goals.

| Tier | Cost | Best For | Lead Time |
|------|------|----------|-----------|
| **Core** | $500–$1,000 | Primary schools, clubs, pilots | 1-2 weeks |
| **FabLab** | $2,500–$5,000 | Middle/High schools, makerspaces | 2-4 weeks |
| **Center** | $10,000+ | Technical schools, district hubs | 6-8 weeks |

## What's Included

### Core ($500-1,000)
- Budget heat press (Transmax/Microtec)
- Hand tools, PPE bundles
- DIY wooden collection boxes
- Manual shredder
- Parchment paper, sandpaper
- **Produces:** Coasters, simple sheets, basic keychains

### FabLab ($2,500-5,000)
- Auto-open heat press (Secabo TC5)
- Bambu Lab A1 / Prusa MK4S 3D printer
- Arduino/ESP32 sensor kits
- GDPR shredder (5-8mm)
- Basic CNC router
- **Produces:** Full product line + custom molds + 3D filament

### Center ($10,000+)
- Premium heat press (Stahls)
- Injection molder (SDS Injection Mini 2)
- Trotec laser cutter
- Precision aluminum molds
- Industrial ventilation system
- Multiple 3D printers
- **Produces:** Architectural tiles, furniture components, B2B orders

## Recommendation

**Start with Core.** Prove the concept, build student engagement, generate revenue. Upgrade to FabLab when you have:
- Consistent 500+ caps/week collection
- $500+/semester product revenue
- 2+ certified teachers
- Admin commitment for expansion

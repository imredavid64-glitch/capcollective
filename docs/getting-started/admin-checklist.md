# Cap Collective — Administrator Quick-Start Checklist
### One-Page Decision Guide for Principals & Department Heads
**Print this. Bring to your leadership meeting.**

---

## THE PITCH (30 SECONDS)
> "Cap Collective is a student-run program that turns bottle cap waste into sellable products. It teaches STEM, entrepreneurship, and sustainability, generates revenue for the school, and gives us grant-worthy measurable impact. We can pilot it for under $1,000."

---

## WHY IT WORKS FOR YOUR SCHOOL
| Benefit | Evidence |
|---------|----------|
| **Curriculum, not a club** | Maps to NGSS/Common Core/IB — 15+ standards |
| **Revenue-generating** | Products sell at 60-85% margin |
| **Grant-magnet** | Measurable data: caps diverted, CO₂e, participation |
| **Low risk** | Certification + SOPs + insurance-ready risk register |
| **Student leadership** | 7 rotating roles, every student engages |
| **Community PR** | Visible recycling, partnerships with local businesses |

---

## DECISION 1: BUDGET TIER
| Tier | Cost | Equipment | Choose if... |
|------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **Core** | $500-1,000 | Heat press, hand tools, PPE, DIY boxes | Primary school, club, pilot |
| **FabLab** | $2,500-5,000 | Auto press, 3D printer, sensors, shredder, CNC | Middle/High, makerspace |
| **Center** | $10,000+ | Premium press, injection molder, laser, molds | Tech school, district hub |

**Recommendation:** Start **Core**. Add FabLab elements when revenue/grants arrive.

---

## DECISION 2: SPACE
**Minimum viable space (Core):**
- One ventilated room (window + exhaust fan)
- 3 work tables
- 1 sink OR portable basins
- Lockable storage
- 16A/20A grounded circuit

**If no dedicated room:** Rotate through science lab / art room / makerspace weekly.

---

## DECISION 3: PEOPLE
| Role | Who | Time |
|------|-----|------|
| **Program Lead** | 1 teacher (stipend ideal) | 2-4 hrs/week |
| **Co-Champion** | 1 more staff (backup) | 2 hrs/week |
| **Student Leads** | 20-40 students | Rotating |
| **Admin Sponsor** | Principal/AP | As needed |

---

## DECISION 4: TIMELINE (18 weeks)
```
W1-2   Launch:     MOU, space, order equipment, recruit leads
W3-4   Build:      Build boxes, sensors, safety certs, first collection
W5-8   Process:    Weekly cycles → 500+ caps, first products
W9-12  Product:    3 product lines, QC, branding
W13-15 Market:     Eco-store launch, community event, report
W16-18 Scale:      Handover, grants, celebrate
```

## RISKS & MITIGATIONS
| Concern | Mitigation |
|---------|------------|
| Safety liability | Certification, SOPs, supervision ratios, risk register, insurance |
| Teacher workload | Ready-made lessons, PD, student leadership, sub-friendly videos |
| Costs overrun | Start Core, revenue funds growth, grant templates provided |
| Low participation | Gamification, leaderboards, assemblies, competitions |
| Equipment failure | Warranty, maintenance schedule, spare parts list |
| No space | Rotating use of existing rooms |

---

## WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU (Admin)
1. **A yes** on the pilot tier and space
2. **A named lead** (with time)
3. **Small seed budget** OR permission to fundraise
4. **Calendar time** for launch week + PD
5. **Communications support** (parents, staff)

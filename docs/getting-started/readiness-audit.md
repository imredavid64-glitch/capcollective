## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Equipment delivery delays | Medium | High | Order early; Core tier uses hand tools as backup |
| Low student sign-up | Low | Medium | Role cards + gamification (see Engagement Guide) |
| Safety incident | Low | Critical | SOPs, 1:4 ratio, certifications, stop-work authority |
| Admin priority shift | Low | High | MOU signed, student leads create momentum |
| Sensor/IoT failures | Medium | Low | Manual entry fallback; CSV import to Sheets |
| Consumable cost overrun | Medium | Medium | Track in Command Center; product revenue offsets |
| Teacher burnout | Medium | High | Rotate leads, sub-ready videos, stipend for Program Lead |

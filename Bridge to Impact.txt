The Solution:
The Bottle Cap Box Project
Why a separate box? Standard recycling plants use huge sorting machines; bottle caps are so small they often fall through the cracks and end up in landfills.
Purity: Mixing different plastics (PET bottles vs. HDPE caps) lowers the quality of recycled products. Separation at the source is the best solution.
Engineering: This isn't just a wooden bin. It is a laser-cut, precision-engineered collection station.
Visual Prompt: The box acts as a "Nudge"—a constant visual reminder of our environmental commitment.


WHY FOCUS ON BOTTLE CAPS?
Separation Needs: Caps are made of different plastic (HDPE) than bottles (PET).
Small but Mighty: Because they are small, they escape standard sorting machines.
Recyclability: Clean bottle caps are highly valuable to specialized recyclers.
Industrialization: Easy to clean, melt, shred, and reuse for many purposes.
Separation Needs: Caps are HDPE/PP; bottles are PET. They cannot be recycled together or they contaminate the batch.
Small but Mighty: Because they are small, they escape standard sorting machines at large plants. Our box captures them at the source.
Recyclability: Clean, sorted caps are highly valuable to specialized recyclers.
Material Purity: Explain that caps are made of HDPE or PP, which are denser and more durable than the PET used in bottles.
The Escapees: Remind the audience that caps are the primary "escapees" of industrial recycling because they are too small for the giant sorting gears.
Recyclability: Clean, source-separated HDPE is the most "upcyclable" material available to us.

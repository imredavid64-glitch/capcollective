Understanding the Challenge

Why Bottle Caps Matter

Industrial mechanical recycling centers process large pieces of plastic like bottles, but small items slip through sorting screens or damage the high-throughput equipment. The Cap Collective focuses entirely on capturing these tiny, valuable resources.

Industrial Blindspot

Most local sorting centers drop smaller items into waste streams due to high-speed sorting grates. Bottle caps are almost universally sent directly to landfills or incinerators despite being perfectly clean, high-grade polymers.

The Premium Plastic Type

Caps are composed of HDPE (#2) and PP (#5). They melt at secure, predictable thermal spectrums, are highly dense, incredibly colorful, structural, food-safe, and do not emit harmful gasses when heated carefully.

Decentralized Micro-Molding

Instead of massive chemical synthesis, we shred the plastic into pristine flakes, heat it uniformly using micro-machinery, and form new structural products like school utensils, tech accessories, and community benches.

How We Recycle Plastic

Phase 01 — Intake Control

Hyper-Local Community Sourcing

Collection begins with custom designated waste drops in educational institutions, including our flagship setup at the American International School of Bucharest (AISB).

  • Material Separation: Users manually unscrew caps, which keeps sticky beverage residues or paper labeling in secondary paths.
  • Washing & Disinfecting: Collected components are soaked in light sanitizer to remove sugars, dust, or organic elements.
  • Sorting by Hue: We separate caps by basic raw colors to generate premium visual marbling on recycled final products.

Phase 02 — Mechanical Breakdown

Shredding into Pristine Flakes

To prepare caps for safe uniform thermo-melting, we must decrease their surface area and volume. The sorted solid units are fed into industrial micro-shredders.

  • High-Torque Rotors: Slowly crush materials into 3mm - 6mm granular flakes without generating frictional burning heat.
  • Sieving Process: Built-in screens ensure raw grains are fully consistent in size before heading to chemical baking stages.
  • Pure Raw Flakes: The result is premium raw colored feedstock resembling tiny beautiful confetti, completely ready to inject.
Processing feedstock granules
Phase 03 — Thermo-Fluid Transformation

Controlled Precision Heating

This is where physics and chemistry unite. The raw polymer flakes are loaded inside our specialized heat chambers to reach structural melting ranges.

  • Stable Temperatures: HDPE melts consistently around 130°C to 180°C. Keeping temperatures below burning points avoids hazardous off-gassing.
  • Micro-Injection: A hydraulic system drives the liquid viscous plastic directly inside solid milled steel or aluminum product molds.
  • High Sheet Presses: For plate goods, heat and pressure compress the melted polymers into dynamic recycled sheet panels.

Phase 04 — Functional Deployment

Deploying Beautiful Products

Once cooled down, the solid mold opens to reveal an exceptionally sturdy, structurally perfect recycled item. No two pieces look identical.

  • Swirled Marble Patterns: The original sorting colors fuse together to create beautiful, ocean-like mineral ripples.
  • Extreme Durability: Since raw caps are built to survive environmental elements, molded products are incredibly impact resistant.
  • Continuous Re-recycling: If an item ever breaks, we can shred and melt it right back into the cycle. 100% loop completion!
